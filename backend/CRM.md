# CRM and student admission

## Access model

There is no CRM role in the current role catalogue. `ADMIN` and `SUPER_ADMIN` can view and manage all leads, assign leads to active staff, and view workload. `TEACHER` users can access only leads assigned to their own account. Lead and trial history is append-only; free-form CRM metadata is not accepted.

## Lead lifecycle

Allowed status changes are enforced in the service and stored in `crm_lead_status_history`. Leads start as `NEW`; `WON` can only be reached by the conversion operation. `WON` and `LOST` are terminal. A loss reason is required. The database prevents duplicate phone numbers among active leads; phone numbers are normalized to E.164. A phone number may be reused after a lead reaches `WON` or `LOST`.

## Admission conversion

Conversion locks the lead and runs user creation/linking, optional group membership, lead status/history, and audit writes in one database transaction. A lead phone alone is never used to link an account. An existing account is eligible only when an already verified `phone` `AuthIdentity` has the same E.164 subject and belongs to an active `STUDENT`. Otherwise, conversion creates an active student with the `STUDENT` role but no password, phone identity, or session; the student must complete the existing verified sign-in flow later. A selected group must be active and belong to the selected course. Repeating conversion returns the already converted student.

## Trials and follow-ups

Trial lessons are CRM records that may refer to an existing scheduled `LessonSession`; this module never creates or modifies group sessions. A linked session must belong to the selected active group and course. Trial status and schedule changes are recorded in `crm_trial_lesson_history`. Follow-up endpoints expose overdue and upcoming work on demand. No reminder scheduler was found in the existing application, so scheduled push reminders are not generated by this module.

## Statistics

Conversion rate is `WON / all leads` as a percentage, with zero for an empty population. Average conversion time is measured in hours from lead creation to conversion. Attended trial count uses trial records in `ATTENDED`. Operator workload reports open and overdue leads grouped by assigned user.

## Database rollout

The additive `20261104220000-CRM` migration creates CRM lead, activity, status history, trial, and trial history tables and indexes. It was not executed. `synchronize` and `migrationsRun` remain disabled.
